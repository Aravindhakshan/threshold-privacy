Nimir — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Nimir is a personal digital-wellbeing app made by Mindful Monkeys. This page explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

Nimir does not collect, store, or send any of your personal data anywhere. Everything the app needs to work stays on your phone, in your phone's storage, and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers in the app.

What the app accesses on your device, and why

  • Usage access — to see when you open an app you chose to lock, so Nimir can show its lock screen. This check happens only on your phone.
  • Display over other apps — to show the lock screen on top of the app you open.
  • Steps and weight (Health Connect, optional) — if you turn on steps, Nimir reads your daily step count to give you unlock time. If you turn on weight tracking, Nimir reads your weight to show your progress. This data stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • Physical activity (optional) — needed by Android to count steps, only if you turn steps on.
  • Notifications — to show that Nimir is running, the Pomodoro timer, and the daily reminder if you turn it on.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional) — so the Pomodoro alarm rings on time.
  • Notification access (optional) — only if you turn on “Auto-restart Nimir”. Android restarts apps with this access if they are stopped, so Nimir keeps working. Nimir does not read, store, or send your notifications.
  • Start at boot and run in the background — so locking keeps working after your phone restarts, without opening the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal identifiers.
  • We don't use accessibility services to read what's on your screen.
  • We don't send any data off your device. Nimir has no internet permission, so it can't connect to the internet at all.
  • We don't share, sell, or otherwise disclose any information, because none is collected in the first place.

Data deletion

Since nothing leaves your device, uninstalling Nimir removes all app data immediately and completely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "last updated" date.
